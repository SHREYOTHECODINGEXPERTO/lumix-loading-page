2. Easing & Timing Physics

All LumiX motions utilize custom cubic-bezier curves mimicking magnetic attraction and aerodynamic lift.

Smooth sinusoidal acceleration at endpoints for alternating lateral motion.

3. Brand Fidelity: Do's & Don'ts

DO

  • Preserve Quadrilateral Angles: Maintain the precise 45° hypotenuse and rounded vertex ratio.
  • Keep Color Pair Separation: Keep `#2870ED` on top-left and `#F07150` on bottom-right.
  • Use 3D Perspective Rotation: Rotate along the diagonal axis to achieve the authentic butterfly effect.
  • Ensure 60+ FPS: Utilize GPU-accelerated CSS `transform` and `opacity` properties.

DON'T

  • Do NOT Distort Aspect Ratio: Never stretch or squash the core geometry independently of perspective.
  • Do NOT Alter Brand Hues: Avoid swapping the blue and coral tones or introducing arbitrary gradients.
  • Do NOT Use Linear Easing: Linear motion feels robotic and contradicts the organic butterfly concept.
  • Do NOT Clip Shadows: Ensure adequate SVG viewBox padding to prevent glow clipping.
